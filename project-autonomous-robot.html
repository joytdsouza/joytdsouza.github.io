Class Project Completed

Autonomous Competition Robot

ME2110 · Atlanta, GA · Sep 2025 – Nov 2025

Picture of the CAD model for the robot we used in the final competition.

Objective

In ME2110: Creative Decisions and Design, groups of 4 work together over 8 weeks to build an autonomous robot with a final bill of materials of $120. Groups are given a mechatronics kit with components such as pneumatic pistons, solenoids, motors, various sensors, and an Arduino Uno. Each team’s robot competes in a final competition, where robots must complete various themed tasks to earn points. These tasks include reaching high objects, manipulating objects to specific positions, and deploying objects at specific times to certain locations. Groups are also responsible for creating technical reports and giving presentations throughout the build process.

Project Description

My team’s robot focused on creating modular subsystems that could easily be interchanged, and minimizing human errors and the need for hand tools through utilizing machine manufacturing methods as much as possible. Before each build iteration, the entire robot was modeled in Onshape, where I exported.svgs and .step files for laser cutting and 3D printing were the main methods of fabrication. I spent a lot of time in the IDEALab - the dedicated studio space for 2110 - laser cutting practice squares on MDF boards and queuing my prints on the BambuLab P1S printers. I also spent a lot of time assembling the robot (we bought our own rachets to speed up assembly). Specific parts of the robot I caded included mounts to hold one of the mounting boards, and to hold the pneumatic piston in the sheep subsystem. I also created two alignment jigs to center the robot on the track.
