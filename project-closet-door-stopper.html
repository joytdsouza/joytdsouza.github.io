Closet Door Stopper

ME1670: Intro to Engr Graphics · Atlanta, GA · Aug 2024 – Dec 2024

Caption goes here — describe what this main image shows.

Objective

For my individual project in ME1670: Intro to Engr Graphics, I had to create a small object that can be printed on the commercial SLS 3D printer (EOS Formiga P110). The object needed 2-4 interlocking parts that each use at least 4 distinct and meaningful CAD operations. Each part’s spatial envelope needed to be less than 3 in. x 3 in. x 3 in, with the total object having a volume less than 3 in3.

Project Description

I wanted to create a practical object, and I knew one of my roommate’s closet doors would not close fully. I designed a sliding hook and clamp mechanism to attach to the top of my roommate’s closet in order to prevent the faulty doors from sliding open. The clamp slots around the top of the closet lip, and the hook moves to block the closet door from opening or moves to the top position to be out of the way.

Reflection

For my first use of any CAD software, I thoroughly enjoyed this project and the planning and design process. While the door stopper did not work because I forgot to account for the weight of the door dislodging the mechanism, I was proud that all of the printed parts fit together, and fit exactly on the closet based on my measurements.
