Force Sensor for 3D-Printed Prosthetic

Medical Robotics Club · Atlanta, GA · Sep 2025 – Present
Arduino Sensor Calibration 3D Printing

FSR voltage response across repeated calibration trials, plotted against known applied loads.

Developing a Force Sensing Resistor (FSR) calibration SOP to standardize sensor variability for integration into a low-cost 3D-printed prosthetic for trans-radial amputees.

  • Calibrating FSRs using known weights and Arduino-collected voltage data to analytically define soft/medium/hard touch thresholds as prosthetic feedback.
  • Designed a 3D-printed force distributor to evenly concentrate applied loads on the sensing surface for repeatable calibration testing.
