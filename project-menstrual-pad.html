Sustainable Menstrual Pad

GT Engineers Without Borders — Wish For WASH · Atlanta, GA · Oct 2024 – Present

Caption goes here — describe what this main image shows.

Objective

At GT’s EWB chapter, Wish For WASH (W4W) is a team focused on projects that tackle the UN’s Sustainable Development Goal 6 related to global water, sanitation, and hygiene. In W4W, I am part of the bioplastics subteam that is developing a sustainable menstrual pad that will be as, if not more, effective compared to current industry products (we use Always as a baseline).

Project Description

Having completed the research portion and determined our materials, based on biodegradability, cost, and availability for pad construction, we moved into material testing. Our testing follows ASTM and ISO standards, and tests for absorptivity, pH, and we will finish up re-wet testing. The majority of the single material testing is finished, and we will move into composite testing based on our findings. Our goal is to have a prototype by the end of this school year.

Reflection

Coming into college, I knew I wanted to join EWB after researching clubs to write about for my application essay. I’ve always wanted to use my college education to help others, and I felt that engineering was the best way for me to do so. EWB and W4W embody this mission, and working on a product that would not only benefit half the population through removing unhealthy materials like plastic from pads, but also our planet through reducing the enormous amount of waste and CO2 generated at the same time, makes me very grateful to be part of this project.
