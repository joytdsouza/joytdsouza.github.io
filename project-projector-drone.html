All-in-One Projector, Popcorn Maker, and Food/Drink Delivery Drone

ME1670: Intro to Engr Graphics · Atlanta, GA · Aug 2024 – Dec 2024

Objective

For my team project in ME1670: Intro to Engr Graphics, I worked with a team of 5 members to sketch, plan, design, model, and document a proposed design project. I was responsible for sketching and modeling a minimum of 5 parts, and collaborating on the team report, including assembly instructions and engineering drawings.

Project Description

My team settled on an all-emcompassing entertainment machine that includes a projector with a popcorn maker and separate drone that delivers food and drink. I designed the snack drone, which docks on top of the popcorn maker, and the top plate and lid of the popcorn maker that contains the finished popcorn attaches to the center of the drone, while the sides of the drone contain compartments for holding drinks or other food. My six components were the drone base with built in drink and snack holders, fan bases to connect the fans to the drone base, motors that sit in the fan base and power the fan, the fan with blades attached to a centerpiece, motor caps to hold the motor in the fan base, and fan caps to prevent the fans from flying off the motor. I also found screws and nuts from McMaster-Carr to fasten the motor cover onto the fan base. The proposed fan base would connect magnetically to the drone base, and this connection would provide connection needed to power the motors.

Reflection

One of the biggest challenges in this project was cohesivenes between each component, especially since everything was done virtually. Not only did I have to ensure my parts fit together, but I needed to make sure they also fit with my teammates’ parts. We learned early on that determining which unit to work in and measurements for adjoining assemblies was crucial. Collaborating simultaneously in the same SolidWorks assembly was also a challenge, and I discovered that having a dedicated team member update one assembly prevented most of the problems we ran into. To meet deliverable deadlines, I took a leadership role to schedule meetings and constantly communicate our progress.
