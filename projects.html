01

Force Sensor for 3D-Printed Prosthetic

Medical Robotics Club · Atlanta, GA · Sep 2025 – Present
  • Developing a Force Sensing Resistor (FSR) calibration SOP to standardize sensor variability for integration into a low-cost 3D-printed prosthetic for trans-radial amputees.
  • Calibrating FSRs using known weights and Arduino-collected voltage data to analytically define soft/medium/hard touch thresholds as prosthetic feedback.
  • Designed a 3D-printed force distributor to evenly concentrate applied loads on the sensing surface for repeatable calibration testing.
Arduino Sensor Calibration 3D Printing
02

Sustainable Menstrual Pad

GT Engineers Without Borders — Wish For WASH · Atlanta, GA · Oct 2024 – Present
  • Collaborating with a 14-member bioplastics team to develop an adhesive-free, sustainable menstrual pad matching the absorbency, comfort, and cost of commercial pads.
  • Conducted single-material absorbance testing on 9 materials per ASTM and ISO standards using water and gelatin blood simulant — identifying Himalayan nettle and wood pulp as top-performing core materials.
  • Progressing to composite and rewet testing across pad layers for absorbency, permeability, and skin comfort.
Materials Testing ASTM / ISO Standards Sustainability
03

Autonomous Competition Robot

ME2110 · Atlanta, GA · Sep 2025 – Nov 2025
  • Collaborated in a 4-member team to design and fabricate a fully autonomous robot in 8 weeks under a $120 budget, competing against 72 teams.
  • Used Onshape, Inkscape, and Bambu Studio for component fabrication on Trotec laser cutters and Bambu Lab P1S printers.
